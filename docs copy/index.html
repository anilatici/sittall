How it works

Up and running
in under a minute.

SitTall - Fix Your Posture is designed to get out of the way. Calibrate once, then forget about it — until you slouch.

  1. 1

    Calibrate once

    Sit up in your best posture and tap "Capture good posture." Then slouch naturally and tap "Capture bad posture." SitTall - Fix Your Posture now knows your personal range.

  2. 2

    Wear your AirPods

    Work normally. SitTall - Fix Your Posture watches silently from the menu bar, tracking forward drift, side tilt, and gradual collapse with the sensors already in your ears.

  3. 3

    Sit up when reminded

    After a configurable period of bad posture, you'll get a gentle nudge. Dismiss, snooze, or just sit up — posture corrected, back to work.
