SitTall - Fix Your Posture turns AirPods into a posture coach. This page is a launch-ready partner brief for creators in health, productivity, and tech.

The product

Your AirPods already know
when you slouch.

A macOS menu bar app that uses your AirPods' built-in motion sensors to detect bad posture and send a gentle nudge. Calibrate once. Works forever. No cameras. No wearables.

Real-time tracking

Forward drift, side tilt, and gradual collapse, detected 60 times per second.

Smart reminders

Alerts only when bad posture persists. Never for a quick glance down.

Daily stats

Slouch count, corrections, health score. Shareable as a daily report.

Fully private

All processing on-device. No account. No data ever leaves your Mac.

How it feels

Quiet in the background. Useful when it matters.

SitTall - Fix Your Posture is designed to stay out of the way until your posture drifts for long enough that a reminder is actually helpful.
