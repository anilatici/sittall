How it works

01

Sit up straight, then slouch. SitTall learns your range in two taps.

02

Put your AirPods in and work normally. SitTall watches from the menu bar.

03

A reminder fires after you've been slouching for a while. Sit up and it goes away.

SitTall runs in your menu bar and reads the 6-axis motion sensors in your AirPods at 60 samples per second. It detects forward head drift, side tilt, and gradual collapse. All processing happens on your Mac — nothing is sent anywhere.

Menu bar native · No camera · No account · No subscription

Nothing leaves your Mac.

SitTall runs inside the macOS App Sandbox with no network entitlement. Motion data from your AirPods is processed locally in real time and never written to disk, transmitted, or shared. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no server to send data to.

Requirements

Mac:     Apple Silicon, macOS 14 Sonoma or later

Frequently asked questions

How does it detect my posture without a camera?

Your AirPods contain a 6-axis inertial measurement unit (accelerometer + gyroscope). SitTall reads the orientation of your head 60 times a second and compares it against the baseline you set during calibration. When your head drifts forward or tilts beyond your threshold for a sustained period, it fires a reminder.

Which AirPods work with SitTall?

AirPods Pro (2nd generation with USB-C or Lightning), AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation, and Beats Fit Pro. These are the models Apple exposes head-tracking motion data for via CoreMotion. Standard AirPods and AirPods Max are not currently supported.

Does it drain my AirPods battery faster?

The motion sensor access adds a small overhead, but most users report no noticeable difference in battery life. The sensors are already active whenever head-tracking spatial audio is on — SitTall reads the same data stream.

Can I use it while listening to music or on calls?

Yes. SitTall only reads motion sensor data and does not interfere with audio playback, microphone input, or noise cancellation. It works alongside any audio app.

Is it really free?

SitTall is free to download with core posture monitoring included. There is no subscription.

What if I recalibrate wrong?

Open SitTall from the menu bar and run calibration again. It takes about ten seconds and you can redo it as often as you like.
